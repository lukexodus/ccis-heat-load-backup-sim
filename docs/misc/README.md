1. `M&S Project Ideas.md`: Original course project concept pitch outlining the CCIS heat, solar, and battery simulation problem statement.
2. `deep-research-report.md` and `deep-research-report (1).md` through `(7).md`: Raw research outputs across 8 topic domains:

   - Topic 4: Rooftop solar PV specifications, NOCT thermal derating, and regional yield targets.
   - Topic 5: Lithium Iron Phosphate (LFP) battery storage chemistry, degradation, and dispatch.
   - Topic 6: Stochastic grid outage modeling, utility reliability metrics, and typhoon coupling.
   - Topic 7: Multi-criteria decision analysis (AHP), Saaty comparison scales, and sizing optimization.
   - Topic 8: Model verification and validation (V&V) standards, physical energy invariants, and ASHRAE Guideline 14 calibration.
